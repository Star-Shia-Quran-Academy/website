Our Journey

The Story of Star Shia Quran Academy

Founded in 2010 by a group of passionate Shia scholars and educators, Star Shia Quran Academy began with a simple yet profound vision: to bring authentic Quranic education to Shia Muslims living in the West and around the world who lacked access to traditional Islamic seminaries.

What started as a small online tutoring initiative has now grown into a globally recognized Shia Quran Academy, serving hundreds of students across 25+ countries. Our team of certified Shia teachers, many of whom hold Ijazah in Quranic recitation and Tafseer, are committed to preserving the rich heritage of Quranic studies within the Shia tradition.

We take pride in our holistic approach, teaching not just the words of the Quran, but its profound meanings and their application in daily life according to the guidance of the Ahlul Bayt (AS).
