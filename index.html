Your Trusted Shia Quran Academy Since 2010

Star Shia Quran Academy is a premier online institution dedicated to providing authentic Quranic education rooted in the teachings of the Ahlul Bayt (AS). We offer structured courses for children, adults, and reverts, blending traditional Islamic pedagogy with modern online tools.
